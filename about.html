About Us

Nike, Inc. is the world's leading designer, marketer, and distributor of athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories, known globally for its iconic "Swoosh" logo and "Just Do It" slogan, named after the Greek goddess of victory, founded by Bill Bowerman and Phil Knight in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports
Nike dominates the sports business, valued as one of the most valuable apparel brands worldwide. Despite controversies, its strong marketing, athlete partnerships, and commitment to innovation keep it at the forefront of the athletic industry, inspiring athletes from professionals to everyday people.
Nike, Inc. (stylized as NIKE) is an American athletic footwear and apparel corporation headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon. It is the world's largest supplier of athletic shoes and apparel and a major manufacturer of sports equipment, with revenue in excess of US$46 billion in its fiscal year 2022.
